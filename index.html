Questions, answered plainly.

What is BioRipple?

A research dependency tool that shows how a change to one artifact may affect connected work downstream.

Does BioRipple prove scientific causation?

No. It maps recorded dependencies. A flagged item is potentially affected and needs scientific review, not automatically invalid.

How does it handle uncertainty?

Every relationship carries a status (verified, supported, inferred, needs review, unverified) and shows whether evidence exists. No probabilities are invented.

Is the demo using real experimental data?

No. Everything here is fictional and illustrative.

Can it replace a laboratory information management system?

No. It sits alongside your records and focuses on dependencies and review.

Can I import data or collaborate with a team?

This preview is a single-page demo. Import, accounts and team roles are not part of it yet.
